A quality upgrade no longer cuts the audio.

### What changed

- **Upgrading a track's stream now crosses over instead of starting over.** When
  the better stream is ready, the old and the new player are ramped across each
  other over 250 ms, on a second player that is already buffered, so the output is
  never torn down and handed to a fresh one. That teardown was the short gap that
  could be heard at the moment the quality changed.
- **The upgrade falls back rather than risk silence.** If playback is paused, or
  already inside a track cross-fade, or the new stream is not ready within six
  seconds, the previous behaviour runs instead. Nothing new is attempted while
  playback is not actually moving.
- **A track played at 1.25x stays at 1.25x across the change.** The rate is copied
  onto the incoming player first, so the cross-over is between two copies of the
  same recording at the same speed rather than smearing through the ramp.

### If you still hear a gap

The log now names which of these happened, so a report can be specific instead of
"it paused":

- `underrun #N: dry for Xms (sink held Yms), buffered ahead=Zms` - the buffer ran dry
- `stalled for Xms, buffered ahead=Yms` - the player stopped advancing
- `paused: another app took audio focus` - expected, not a bug
- `swap blend abandoned for <id>: <reason>` - the upgrade took the fallback path,
  and the reason says which guard stopped it
